About

On the work.

Six years keeping systems alive, most of them as the lone technologist for the Canadian arm of a global firm. The kind of role that teaches three habits: design for the moment things break, write everything down, and automate anything not worth doing twice.

Lately the work has been moving cloudward, deliberately. Certifications earned, two production-grade projects shipped, side projects built with the same habits: infrastructure as code, pipelines, and a clean split between dev and prod.

After hours: Canadian soccer, followed with unreasonable devotion. Hence the tracker, a site that wakes each night to take the national team's pulse.

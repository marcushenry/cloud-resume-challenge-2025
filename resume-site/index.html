About

About me.

I’m an IT operations professional moving deeper into cloud and infrastructure work.

For the past four years, I’ve been the main local IT professional for the Canadian side of a global company. That means a bit of everything: troubleshooting, onboarding, access, hardware, SaaS admin, documentation, vendors, and keeping people working when something breaks.

Lately I’ve been spending more of my time building cloud projects and polishing my automation skills. I’ve worked with AWS, Terraform, GitHub Actions, CI/CD pipelines, scheduled jobs, monitoring, and proper dev/prod separation. I plan on continuing to develop my skills every day, and using them practically as much as possible.

Outside of that, I follow Canadian soccer with a very keen eye, which is how the CMNT Squad Tracker project started.
